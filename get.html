Download Aloe Scribe

It records your meetings and writes the transcript, speaker names and summary on your own computer. No account, and nothing to buy to download it.

Mac

Apple Silicon (M1 or later), macOS 13 or newer, 16 GB of memory recommended.

Windows

64-bit Windows 10 or 11, 16 GB of memory recommended. No admin rights needed.

Download for Windows

Version 1.3.48, about 1.8 GB. If Windows SmartScreen warns, click More info, then Run anyway.
