Privacy policy

What we collect, and what we never see

Aloe Scribe runs on your computer, and your meetings stay there. This page lists the few things that do reach us or our providers, and why.

Effective November 27, 2026

What we never receive

Your audio, transcripts, summaries, notes, Ask notes questions and voice profiles stay on your computer. Aloe Scribe LLC never receives them, never stores them and never uses them to train anything. The app sends no meeting content to us. It never phones home, and it only goes online when you ask it to, for the downloads and checks listed below.

Voice profiles

When you name a speaker, Aloe Scribe saves a voice fingerprint on your computer so it can label that person in later meetings. It is kept in a file named speakers.json in the .local/share/aloe-scribe folder inside your home folder, on Mac and on Windows. Delete that file to delete every saved voice. We never receive it. Only save the voice of someone who has agreed to it.

AI apps and your own model server

Both of these are off until you turn them on. If you connect an AI app such as Claude or ChatGPT to your notes, the passages it reads go to that app’s provider under its own terms. If you point summaries or Ask notes at a model server that is not on your computer, that server receives the text it works on. The built-in model keeps everything on your computer.

Email

We email only people who signed up, and only about what they signed up for. Every email includes our postal address and a way to unsubscribe, and any reply unsubscribes you.

Your choices and rights

Write to feedback@aloescribe.ai to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, or to come off the email list. We answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU or the UK, we rely on your consent for the email list and on our contract with you for a license, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Aloe Scribe and this site are not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the release notes will say so.
