Aloe Scribe

Get your license

Paste the key from your receipt, or the code someone gave you. This page turns it into the license you paste into Aloe Scribe. The app checks it on your computer and never goes online for it.

Lost it? The same key gets the same license again, as many times as you need.

Each code works once, so copy your license when it appears. The email is only for sending a lost license again. Nothing else is sent to it.

Your license

  1. In Aloe Scribe, open Settings.
  2. Under License, choose Add license.
  3. Paste it and choose Add.

The same license works on your Mac and your Windows PC. Keep a copy with your receipt.
